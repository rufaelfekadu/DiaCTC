How the lexicon changes decoding

Arabic normally leaves out its short vowels. The letters كتب (k-t-b) can be read katab–, kutib– or kutub–, and the decoder restores the marks from the audio.

Each slot below shows how likely the audio makes each mark. Pick a scenario, set the weight, and press Play to watch both decoders choose.

Without the lexicon

One chain. After each letter the decoder may pick any mark, so it takes whichever the audio makes most likely.

With the lexicon

The same chain is still there, but entering it costs the weight. Beside it run the readings the lexicon lists, at no cost. The best total wins.
